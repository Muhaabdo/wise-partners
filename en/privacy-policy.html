1Introduction

Wise Partners is an independent media and marketing platform, not a real estate developer. The platform is owned and operated by أحمد صبحي عبدالشافي السيد as an independent real estate marketer who promotes projects in coordination with developers, without exclusive mandate unless expressly stated.

This policy applies to wisepartners-eg.com and all related forms and contact channels (WhatsApp and phone calls). By using the site or submitting your details, you agree to the collection and use of your data as described here.

2Data we collect

Data you provide: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email if provided, and details of your enquiry, the project you are interested in, budget and preferences.

Non-personal technical data collected automatically through analytics and advertising tools: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, traffic source and campaign identifiers (such as gclid and UTM).

We do not collect any payment or bank card data through the site.

3How we use your data

  • To answer your enquiries and provide suitable real estate advice.
  • To send project information, prices and availability when you ask.
  • To improve the site and measure advertising performance.
  • To meet legal requirements.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for third-party marketing without your explicit consent.

4Legal basis for processing

We process your data based on your explicit consent when you submit a form or contact us, and on our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry and operating the site. You may withdraw consent at any time via the contact options in section 14.

5Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell your data. Sharing is limited to:

  • The platform's internal sales team.
  • The developer of the project you enquire about, with your consent, limited to name and phone number.
  • Technical service providers (hosting and tools) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Advertising and analytics platforms such as Google.
  • Authorities where required by law.

6Cookies and advertising

We use essential cookies to run the site, analytics cookies to measure visits, and marketing tools such as Google Ads and Google Tag Manager to measure conversions and remarketing.

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Google account settings.

7Data retention

  • Non-contracted enquirers: up to 24 months from the last interaction, unless you request deletion earlier.
  • Contracted clients: for the duration of the contract and up to 10 years after for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Analytics data is kept anonymised; after the retention period data is deleted or anonymised.

8Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, object to marketing use, and request restriction of processing.

We respond within 30 working days of receiving your request.

9Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including HTTPS encryption, restricting access to authorised people, periodic review and trusted service providers. No transmission or storage method over the internet is 100% secure.

10Children's privacy

The site is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If we learn that data was collected from a minor without parental consent, we delete it immediately.

11Limitation of liability

The platform is not liable for damage caused by events beyond its reasonable control, such as unforeseen breaches, third parties failing their obligations, outages of external platforms, or inaccurate data entered by users.

Prices and information shown are indicative; the developer and official contracts are the final reference.

12Governing law and disputes

This policy is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt, primarily Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020. We aim to settle disputes amicably first; otherwise Egyptian courts have jurisdiction.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date; please review it periodically.

14Privacy contact

For any question or request about your data, contact Wise Partners (أحمد صبحي عبدالشافي السيد) by phone or WhatsApp and mention that your request concerns privacy.
